This policy explains what personal information {{ business:name }} collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you use our contact form or apply for a role through our careers pages, we collect the information you submit, such as your name, email address, message, the role you are applying for, and any documents you attach (for example, a résumé). We use it only to respond to you and, for applications, to evaluate your candidacy. Applications are reviewed by our recruiting team and are not sold or shared for marketing.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors arrive. Analytics cookies are off by default and are only set if you choose “Accept” in the cookie banner. If you choose “Decline”, we do not set analytics cookies.

Your choice is remembered in your browser’s local storage so we do not ask you again. You can change it at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer, or by clearing your browser data.

Google processes analytics data under its own privacy terms. You can also opt out across all sites with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

How long we keep information

We keep contact messages and job applications only as long as needed for the purpose they were submitted, or as required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, and to request that we correct or delete it. To make a request, use the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
